Why do most people end up quitting training?

The biggest reason: they start too hard and too fast.

When you try to be “perfect” with workouts and diet from day one, you burn out in 1–2 weeks and eventually give up.

EUNBODY’s goal is not a 1-week transformation, but habits you can keep long-term.

More than half of your results come from nutrition.

To build muscle and lose fat efficiently, you need:

  • Daily calories that match your goal
  • Enough protein for muscle repair and growth
